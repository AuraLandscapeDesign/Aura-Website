About AURA

Design rooted in
place & purpose.

AURA Landscape Design was born from a belief that every outdoor space holds potential to tell a story. Founded by Daniel Knott — a qualified landscape designer and construction expert with over six years of hands-on industry experience across Melbourne — AURA combines rigorous design thinking with real-world construction expertise.

From intimate garden transformations to expansive estate landscapes, every project begins with careful listening and ends with spaces that feel inevitable — as though they were always meant to be.

What We Offer

A complete
design service.

A full-service design studio offering everything from initial consultation through to a complete landscape design package — choose any combination of services to suit your project and vision.

01
✦

Design Consultation

Expert guidance sessions for homeowners seeking professional input on their existing garden or upcoming project.

02
◕

Site Analysis

A formal assessment of your site’s soil, drainage, aspect, and existing features — laying the groundwork for a design that truly belongs to its place.

03
◈

Concept Design

Presentation-ready concept designs, presented and walked through with you for your approval.

04
⬢

Landscape Design Set

Tailored designs developed through Vectorworks, delivering detailed plans, elevations, construction detailing, and 3D visualisations.

05
◎

Planting Design

Curated plant palettes selected for appearance, site conditions, water availability and long-term resilience.

06
◇

Project Management

End-to-end coordination of trades, materials and timelines — ensuring your project is delivered on time, to the highest standard and on budget.
