About AURA

Design rooted in
place & purpose.

AURA Landscape Design was born from a belief that every outdoor space holds potential to tell a story. Founded by Daniel Knott — a qualified landscape designer and construction expert with over six years of hands-on industry experience across Melbourne — AURA combines rigorous design thinking with real-world construction expertise.

From intimate garden transformations to expansive estate landscapes, every project begins with careful listening and ends with spaces that feel inevitable — as though they were always meant to be.
